# Omoniyi Akinnusi — GTM Engineering Portfolio

A responsive, dark, premium personal website positioning Omoniyi for GTM Engineering roles, project collaborations, and client opportunities.

## Included
- Home, About, What I Do, Projects, Approach, Tools, and Contact sections
- Responsive mobile navigation
- WhatsApp, LinkedIn, and email contact links (placeholders must be replaced)
- Contact form that opens the visitor's email app with a pre-filled message
- SEO title, meta description, canonical URL, Open Graph and Twitter metadata
- Person structured data (JSON-LD)
- `robots.txt`, `sitemap.xml`, accessible navigation and reduced-motion support

## Before publishing — required edits
Search all files for `YOUR-` and replace:
1. `YOUR-DOMAIN.example` with your final domain, including in `index.html`, `robots.txt`, and `sitemap.xml`.
2. `2348064066706` with your international phone number in digits only, including country code. For Nigeria, it normally starts with `234`, not `0`; do not include `+`, spaces, or hyphens.
3. `omoniyi-akinnusi-b7918743b` with your actual LinkedIn profile slug.
4. `Omoniyiakinnusi@gmail.com` with the email address you want to receive messages. Update it in both `index.html` and `script.js`.

Also:
- Replace the project concepts/statuses with your real work as you complete it. Do not present planned projects as finished results.
- Add a professional portrait and a social preview image before launch if desired; update the Open Graph image URL.
- Review the About copy for personal accuracy.
- The current form uses `mailto:` and opens the visitor's email app; for direct website delivery without requiring the visitor's email app, connect a form backend/email service and add spam protection. The current form uses `mailto:` and does not store submissions.

## SEO launch checklist
- Use one permanent HTTPS domain and replace all placeholder URLs.
- Keep one clear page title and meta description.
- Verify the site in Google Search Console and submit `/sitemap.xml`.
- Create a real social preview image (recommended 1200 × 630).
- Compress any photos and provide descriptive alt text.
- Add relevant, accurate case studies with real outcomes; avoid unsupported claims.
- Add a privacy notice if you introduce analytics, tracking, or server-side form processing.
- SEO cannot be guaranteed as “perfect”; indexing and rankings depend on competition, content quality, site performance, links, and search-engine decisions.

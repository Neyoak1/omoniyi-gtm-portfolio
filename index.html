01 / ABOUT ME

I'm interested in the how behind growth.

Not just how a business gets attention—but how it builds a system that turns the right signals into meaningful conversations and measurable progress.

I'm Omoniyi Akinnusi, an aspiring GTM Engineer exploring how data, technology, and automation can make go-to-market work more focused and repeatable.

My approach combines analytical thinking with a builder's mindset. I enjoy breaking a business problem into smaller parts, understanding the information behind it, and designing a practical workflow that can be tested and improved.

I'm currently developing my GTM Engineering practice through hands-on learning and projects involving prospect research, buying signals, enrichment, lead qualification, AI-assisted personalization, workflow automation, and performance measurement.

I believe good systems should do more than look impressive: they should solve a real problem, respect the people using them, and make their impact easier to understand.
